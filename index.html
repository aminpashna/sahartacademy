02 — About

Sahar makes work you can walk around and live with.

Working across painting and sculpture, Sahar builds pieces that hold colour and weight in the same breath — flat fields that quietly turn dimensional, forms that catch the light differently as you move past them. Her studio, Sah Art Academy, is both where the work is made and where she teaches others to find their own line and form.

She takes a limited number of commissions each year and welcomes studio visits by appointment.
